# I&A Parfums storefront — repo rules

Static React storefront (Vite + Tailwind) on GitHub Pages; Shopify will be the commerce backend, as on iana-schoolwear, iana-fashions and iana-tailoring.

## Rules
1. **Facts come from `src/data/site.ts`; scents from `src/data/scents.json`.** Never invent prices, delivery or returns terms, the perfumer, materials, concentration, longevity, notes or ingredient claims. Confirmed (in `SITE`): bottles from £150; delivery £4.95 standard (5 working days), £7.95 express (48 hours), free from £100, click and collect in Tooting; 28-day returns. The 2ml sample price is `£[SAMPLE PRICE]`. Unconfirmed items go in `SITE.toConfirm` and appear on pages only as `[A LABELLED PLACEHOLDER]`. While `SITE.preview` is true, scents are samples and must stay labelled as such: four placeholders, one per family, with working names Wandle (Fresh), Furzedown (Floral), Common (Woody) and Mitcham (Warm), listed in `toConfirm`, with notes shown only as `[NOTES TO COME]` until the client sends them. No placeholder brackets in headings or display text: body size only. Never use a real fragrance house's product name.
2. **Commerce goes through `src/lib/store.ts` only.** Components never read the data file directly. The scent finder's ranking lives in `findScents()` there.
3. **Every page sets `<Seo>`.** Add new routes to `scripts/prerender.mjs`.
4. **Imagery is drawn in code** (`src/components/Art.tsx`): one bottle construction with a proportion per family (`SHAPES`: tall Fresh, rounded Floral, square Woody, squat Warm) and the scent's name on the label, the 2ml vial, the hero still life, the discovery box, the gift box, the seasonal box and labelled photo slots. Flat fills from the palette, 1.5px strokes with `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke`, no gradients, no numbered markers, no decorative pinstripes. No stock or made-up photography; no faces.
5. **Brand (I&A guide 2022, Parfums variant):** Ochre #45353a is the ground for hero, scent panels and footer (#2a2326 deep); warm white #fbfbf9 for reading; Sage #d8e2dc for notes diagrams and bands; Light Pink #ffcad4 only as a faint highlight, never text. Cormorant Garamond Bold headings (sentence case); Agrandir body and 0.15em uppercase labels, Lexend until licensed (change `--font-sans` only). Corners 3px. No pills, gradients as decoration, or card grids for prose. Darker and quieter than Fashions; softer than Tailoring.
6. **Smell it first is the core idea:** every scent is offered as a 2ml sample before the bottle, the sample price is credited against the 50ml/100ml, and the shopper's "I have the sample" state and scent-finder answers (saved on the device, `src/lib/prefs.tsx`) are respected on every page. The sample credit is our proposal ("Proposed" in `toConfirm`). The full finder widget lives on Home and `/scent-finder` only; other pages link to it. Other ways to buy: discovery set, gift box (`/gift-box`) and the seasonal edition (`/seasonal-edition`, register interest saved on the device only).
7. Tap targets ≥ 44px, no horizontal overflow at 375px, body text ≥ 16px, AA contrast with solid tones, British English, GBP.

## Design system (v3 pilot: Home and the scent page)
- **Type:** fluid steps in `index.css` (`.t-hero`, `.t-name`, `.t-h1`, `.t-h2`, `.t-h3`, `.t-lede`); never hard-code a heading size on these pages. `.voice` (Cormorant italic) is the short second line under a name or heading; caps `.label` is for controls and footer headings only, not eyebrows above headings.
- **Motion:** tokens `--ease-out`, `--dur-1/2/3`. Motion answers an action (choosing a bottle, choosing a size, opening a scent). The one unprompted moment is the hero shelf arriving (`.rise`). Reduced motion switches all of it off.
- **Home hero is the range:** four bottles on one shelf (`Shelf` in `Home.tsx`); the chosen bottle steps forward, the ground takes its tint (`mix(Ochre, scent colour, 0.09)`), its details read beside it. It opens on the shopper's finder pick.
- **Scent page:** the top section takes the scent's light tint; the stage shows the size you chose (vial forward for 2ml, bottle for 50ml and 100ml); the buy panel reads the price for that size. Placeholders stay at body size.
- **Shared elements:** links to a scent use `VLink` and the bottle carries `bottleVT(handle)`, so the bottle on a shelf or card becomes the bottle on its page.
- **Header** is Ochre with the white lockup on every page; the strip above it is the deep tone.
