# I&A Parfums storefront — repo rules

Static React storefront (Vite + Tailwind) on GitHub Pages; Shopify will be the commerce backend, as on iana-schoolwear, iana-fashions and iana-tailoring.

## Rules
1. **Facts come from `src/data/site.ts`; scents from `src/data/scents.json`.** Never invent prices, delivery or returns terms, the perfumer, materials, concentration, longevity, notes or ingredient claims. Confirmed (in `SITE`): bottles from £150; delivery £4.95 standard (5 working days), £7.95 express (48 hours), free from £100, click and collect in Tooting; 28-day returns. The 2ml sample price is `£[SAMPLE PRICE]`. Unconfirmed items go in `SITE.toConfirm` and appear on pages only as `[A LABELLED PLACEHOLDER]`. While `SITE.preview` is true, scents are samples and must stay labelled as such: four placeholders, one per family, with working names Wandle (Fresh), Furzedown (Floral), Common (Woody) and Mitcham (Warm), listed in `toConfirm`, with notes shown only as `[NOTES TO COME]` until the client sends them. No placeholder brackets in headings or display text: body size only. Never use a real fragrance house's product name.
2. **Commerce goes through `src/lib/store.ts` only.** Components never read the data file directly. The scent finder's ranking lives in `findScents()` there.
3. **Every page sets `<Seo>`.** Add new routes to `scripts/prerender.mjs`.
4. **Imagery is drawn in code** (`src/components/Art.tsx`): one bottle construction with a proportion per family (`SHAPES`: tall Fresh, rounded Floral, square Woody, squat Warm) and the scent's name on the label, the 2ml vial, the hero still life, the discovery box, the gift box, the seasonal box and labelled photo slots. Flat fills from the palette, 1.5px strokes with `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke`, no gradients, no numbered markers, no decorative pinstripes. No stock or made-up photography; no faces.
5. **Brand (I&A guide 2022 palette, Parfums variant):** Ochre #45353a is the ground for hero, scent panels and footer (#2a2326 deep); warm white #fbfbf9 for reading; Sage #d8e2dc for bands; Light Pink #ffcad4 only as a faint highlight, never text. Type is this shop's own: **Bodoni Moda** for display (weight 500, optical sizing on, sentence case; 600 only at the two small steps; the italic is the second voice) and **Jost** 400/500 for text, controls and the few caps labels (0.15em). Change `--font-serif` / `--font-sans` only; text drawn in `Art.tsx` names the same two families. The logo lockups are outlined SVG and do not change. Corners 3px. No pills, gradients as decoration, or card grids for prose. Darker and quieter than Fashions; softer than Formal.
6. **Smell it first is the core idea:** every scent is offered as a 2ml sample before the bottle, the sample price is credited against the 50ml/100ml, and the shopper's "I have the sample" state and scent-finder answers (saved on the device, `src/lib/prefs.tsx`) are respected on every page. The sample credit is our proposal ("Proposed" in `toConfirm`). The full finder widget lives on Home and `/scent-finder` only; other pages link to it. Other ways to buy: discovery set, gift box (`/gift-box`) and the seasonal edition (`/seasonal-edition`, register interest saved on the device only).
7. Tap targets ≥ 44px, no horizontal overflow at 375px, body text ≥ 16px, AA contrast with solid tones, British English, GBP.

## Design system (v3, every page)
- **Type:** one fluid scale, defined once as `--t-*` in `index.css` (hero, name, h1, h2, h3, h4, lede, small; body is 17px). `h1` and `h2` take their step by default; use `.t-hero`, `.t-name`, `.t-h1` … `.t-h4`, `.t-lede`, `.t-small` elsewhere. Never hard-code a heading size in a page. `.voice` (Bodoni italic) is the short second line under a name or heading; `.fig` sets a price in the display face. Caps `.label` is for control legends, table heads, footer headings and the Preview tag only: no eyebrows above headings, no middle-dot strings, no arrows on links.
- **Motion:** tokens `--ease-out`, `--dur-1/2/3`. Motion answers an action (choosing a bottle, a size, opening a scent, opening the menu). The one unprompted moment is the Home shelf arriving (`.rise`). Reduced motion switches all of it off.
- **The shelf is the signature** (`.shelf-btn`, `.tint`): bottles stand on one ruled line, the chosen one steps forward and the ground takes its tint. Home hero (`Shelf` in `Home.tsx`, Ochre ground, opens on the finder pick); the range (all four on one line from 768px, two by two on phones; pointer or focus steps a bottle forward; a family page opens with that family's bottle forward; no sidebar); the finder result (`ScentFinder`: the picks step forward, the rest step back, the band takes the tint); the gift box chooser; the 404. Light pages tint with `mix(paper, scent colour, 0.3)`.
- **Scent page:** the top section takes the scent's tint; the stage shows the size you chose (vial forward for 2ml, bottle for 50ml and 100ml); the buy panel reads the price for that size. Placeholders stay at body size.
- **Phone bar** (`StickyBar`): on the scent page, discovery set and gift box, the chosen size, its price line and the call action sit at the bottom while the real buttons are out of view; it steps aside for the real buttons and the footer.
- **Shared elements:** links to a scent use `VLink` and the bottle carries `bottleVT(handle)`. Where one page draws the same bottle twice (Home: shelf and finder result) the second uses `VLink carry` with `data-carry`, so only one element holds the name.
- **Layout:** `.wrap` is 1320px; full-bleed bands carry the edges. Pages open with crumbs, the title and its lede in an asymmetric split; ruled term-and-meaning lists use `.rows`. A page that ends on a band gets `.end-band` on it so it meets the footer.
- **Header** is Ochre with the white lockup on every page; the strip above it is the deep tone. The phone menu is a full panel (`Menu` in `Layout.tsx`): nav, phone number, address, hours. The footer leads with the sample line and the delivery line, then four columns and the legal line.
- **Forms:** labels above fields, 17px inputs, errors say what to fix (`SeasonalEdition`).
- `scripts/prerender.mjs` serves `dist/` on any free port.
