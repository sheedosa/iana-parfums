# I&A Parfums storefront — repo rules

Static React storefront (Vite + Tailwind) on GitHub Pages; Shopify will be the commerce backend, as on iana-schoolwear, iana-fashions and iana-tailoring.

## Rules
1. **Facts come from `src/data/site.ts`; scents from `src/data/scents.json`.** Never invent prices, delivery or returns terms, the perfumer, materials, concentration, longevity, notes or ingredient claims. Confirmed (in `SITE`): bottles from £150; delivery £4.95 standard (5 working days), £7.95 express (48 hours), free from £100, click and collect in Tooting; 28-day returns. The 2ml sample price is `£[SAMPLE PRICE]`. Unconfirmed items go in `SITE.toConfirm` and appear on pages only as `[A LABELLED PLACEHOLDER]`. While `SITE.preview` is true, scents are samples and must stay labelled as such: four placeholders, one per family, with working names Wandle (Fresh), Furzedown (Floral), Common (Woody) and Mitcham (Warm), listed in `toConfirm`, with notes shown only as `[NOTES TO COME]` until the client sends them. No placeholder brackets in headings or display text: body size only. Never use a real fragrance house's product name, and do not cite other houses on the site. Say each placeholder once on a page, not on every row.
2. **Commerce goes through `src/lib/store.ts` only.** Components never read the data file directly. The scent finder's ranking lives in `findScents()` there. Which scent a page is showing lives in `src/lib/stage.tsx` (`useStage`).
3. **Every page sets `<Seo>`.** Add new routes to `scripts/prerender.mjs`.
4. **Imagery is drawn in code** (`src/components/Art.tsx`), on dark: one house bottle for every scent (glass wall and thick base, liquid line, dip tube, brass collar, lacquer cap, paper label with the scent's name) in that scent's colour, the 2ml vial (its name written up the label) and the gift box. Lettering in the drawings is Jost capitals, tracked; SVG adds tracking after the last letter, so a centred line is moved right by half its tracking. Flat tones, no gradients. No stock or made-up photography, no faces, no photo slots. The social card is built from the same drawings by `/home/claude/builds/v5/iana-parfums/_scripts/og.mjs`.
5. **Brand (v4, the gallery wall):** dark on every page. Deep `#2a2326` is the ground, Ochre `#45353a` appears only inside drawings, Bone `#ede6dc` is the text, `#b8aba4` the secondary text, Amber `#c98a3c` the one action on a view (`.act`). Each scent washes the whole page: `wash(colour)` gives a dark tone of its hue at the lightness of Deep, so Bone, secondary text and Amber keep AA on it; never put a light, Sage or pink ground on a page. Type (v5): **Jost only, no serif anywhere** (the other I&A shops own the serif, rounded, engraved, slab and condensed letters; this one owns hairline geometric capitals). Scent names and h1 are UPPERCASE and tracked: weight 200 at display sizes (the name across the width, h1 from 1024px), 300 at text sizes and for h1 on phones, 400 in the dock and for the small names under bottles and vials on phones. Tracking falls as size grows: `--track-name` .16em for h1 and names (.14em for h1 on desktop, .12em for `.name-sm`), `--track-fit` .06em for the full-width name. h2 is 300 and h3 400, sentence case, never capitals. Reading text is Jost 400 at 17px, 500 for the action and strong. Use `.name` for a scent's name in text, `.name-c` when it is centred, `.name-sm` under a bottle or vial. Do not add a serif, a second family, italics, bold display or capitals for sentences longer than a title. Change `--font-display` / `--font-sans` only; text drawn in `Art.tsx` and the social card name the same family. `-webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased` stays on `body` so the thin strokes do not shimmer. The white lockup is outlined SVG and does not change.
6. **No boxes.** No cards, panels, bordered buttons, chips, pills or ruled tables. Options and facts are rows (`src/components/Rows.tsx`): a term, a dotted leader, the value at the right, an Amber underline on the chosen one (`PickRow`, `aria-pressed`). Form fields are a line to write on. The only solid line on a page is the shelf in the dock.
7. **Sample first is the core idea:** every scent is offered as a 2ml sample before the bottle, the sample price is credited against the 50ml/100ml, and the shopper's "I have tried the 2ml sample" state and scent-finder answers (saved on the device, `src/lib/prefs.tsx`) are respected on every page. The sample credit is our proposal ("Proposed" in `toConfirm`). The finder lives on `/scent-finder` only; other pages link to it. Other ways to buy (discovery set, gift box at `/gift-box`, seasonal edition at `/seasonal-edition` with register interest saved on the device only) are reached from the "Other ways to buy" rows (`OtherWays`), never from cards or bands.
8. Tap targets ≥ 44px, no horizontal overflow at 375px, body text ≥ 16px (17px), AA contrast with solid tones, one `h1` per page, British English, GBP. There is no cart: do not add a bag.

## Design system (v4, every page)
- **Voice:** quiet and factual. Say what a thing is; no slogans, no three-part headlines, no "not X but Y". One sentence per scent, built from the data (`scentLine`), and nothing we do not know.
- **Header:** the white lockup at the left, "Samples" (`/discovery-set`) and "Visit" at the right, nothing else, no strip, not sticky, no ground of its own.
- **The dock is the navigation and the signature** (`Dock` in `Layout.tsx`, `.dock`): the four bottles on the one shelf line, fixed along the bottom of every page. The scent the page is showing steps forward and its name is underlined; the finder's first pick is marked "Your pick" on the shelf. `body` carries bottom padding of `--dock-h` so the dock never covers an action or the footer; below 768px it shrinks to small bottles and names.
- **Wash:** a page calls `useStage(handle)` (or `useStage(null)` for the plain Deep ground). The provider sets `--wash` on `<html>`; body and dock take it. Home sets it from the screen in view.
- **Home:** an h1 sentence, then one scent per screen (`Screen`): the name across the width (`FitName`, which measures the word in Jost 200 capitals, adds the tracking between letters, and sizes it with container units; the CSS takes back the tracking after the last letter with a negative margin, and `TRACK` in `FitName.tsx` must equal `--track-fit`), the bottle, one line, "Sample it". Then how the sample works in three sentences with the two bill lines (`Slip`), then "Other ways to buy".
- **The range** (`/range`, `/range/:family`) has no grid: all four bottles on one line with names beneath; a family page stands that family's bottle forward and takes its wash.
- **Scent page:** the name at display size, the bottle with its vial (the chosen size stands forward), sizes as price rows, the sample prices labelled once, the "I have tried the 2ml sample" row, the call action, one delivery sentence, the note layers with `[NOTES TO COME]` once at body size, "Wear it with" and "Other ways to buy" as rows.
- **Footer:** two short blocks of small text above the dock: the legal, address, preview and delivery sentence, then a line of links.
- **Type:** `--t-h1`, `--t-h2`, `--t-h3`, `--t-lede`, `--t-small` in `index.css`; h1/h2/h3 take their step by default. Never hard-code a heading size in a page; the two exceptions are the Home h1 (a body-size sentence in sentence case) and the names under the range and the vials (`.name-sm`), which shrink to fit four across on a phone.
- **Motion:** tokens `--ease-out`, `--dur-1/2/3`. Motion answers an action (the wash changing as you scroll or choose, a bottle stepping forward, the page change). The one unprompted moment is the first Home bottle arriving (`.rise`). Reduced motion switches all of it off.
- **Shared elements:** links to a scent use `VLink`; the page's large bottle carries `bottleVT(handle)`, one holder per page. Dock bottles do not carry it; the dock keeps its own `view-transition-name`.
- **Forms:** labels above fields, 17px inputs, errors say what to fix (`SeasonalEdition`).
- `scripts/prerender.mjs` serves `dist/` on any free port.
